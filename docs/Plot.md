#Story of Asteroid Boy
##Part One
- Black screen with text that says _"Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you"_ (Ephesians 5:14)
- Bob wakes up in a room next to a broken stasis chamber, around him flash alert lights and broken consoles. "Where... where am I?"
- Bob walks up to a torn piece of paper, and the paper says _"But God shows His love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."_ (Romans 5:8). Bob says, "Christ... I've heard that name."
- Bob walks up to another verse that says _"For by you I can run against a troop, and by my God I can leap over a wall."_ (Psalm 18:29). A dialogue window pops up that tells Bob he can jump and run up walls now. Bob cannot leave the section of the station because of fire ahead of him, but he can run up the opposing wall, onto the ceiling, and then down and past the fire by running along the ceiling above. 
- Bob comes to a hull breach, and can only get across by pulling himself to meteors between parts of the ship. A verse in front of him reads _"He drew me up from the pit of destruction, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock."_ (Psalm 40:2). Bob can now use a pull beam to pull himself to different planetoids. Bob uses the pullbeam to pull himself to different planets in the chasm between one section of space station and the other.
